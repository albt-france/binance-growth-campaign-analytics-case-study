3 recommendations

  1. Credit the campaign with +5% at most, not +65%: regions without the campaign rose almost as much (market volume on the most volatile BTC day of the window).
  2. Judge trading competitions against untreated regions or a holdout of eligible users.
  3. If repeated, randomise eligibility (e.g. 10% holdout) so the effect can be read in days.

For operations

  • Show the control regions next to the treated region in the campaign view.
  • Annotate the dashboard with the market context (BTC volume, volatility) of each campaign day.

For executives

  • Trades rose +65% during the competition, but +5% once the market is taken out (95% CI +2% to +9%).
  • Most of the rise came from the market, not the campaign.
  • Next competitions will keep a holdout so their effect can be measured.
